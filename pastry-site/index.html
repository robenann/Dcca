Our Story

Where Ethiopian coffee meets French pastry.

Hammer was built on two simple things done properly: a great cup of coffee and a pastry worth sitting down for. Our beans come from Ethiopia's best growing regions, and our pastry kitchen starts before sunrise so everything on the counter is fresh that day.

Come for a quick macchiato on the way to work, or stay the afternoon with friends. There's always a table for you in Bole.

  • Ethiopian single-origin beans
  • Baked fresh every morning
  • Real butter, no shortcuts
  • Custom cakes for every occasion
