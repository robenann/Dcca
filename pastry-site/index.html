Our Story

A small kitchen with a long memory.

Maison Miel began as a single oven and a family recipe for honey madeleines. Today our team of pastry chefs still works the same way: small batches, seasonal fruit from local growers, and no shortcuts on technique.

Everything on the counter is made in-house that morning. Whatever's left at close goes to the community food bank down the street.

  • 🌾 Locally milled flour
  • 🧈 Cultured European-style butter
  • 🍓 Seasonal, regional fruit
  • ♻️ Zero-waste closing policy
